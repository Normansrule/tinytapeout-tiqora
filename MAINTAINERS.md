# Maintainers

| Maintainer | Role |
|---|---|
| [@Normansrule](https://github.com/Normansrule) (Aleksander J. Norman) | sole maintainer: reviews and merges every change, owns releases and Tiny Tapeout submissions |

`.github/CODEOWNERS` assigns every file to @Normansrule, so GitHub asks for their review on
every pull request.

Questions, bug reports and proposals go to this repository's GitHub issues. Pull requests
are welcome; the maintainer reviews them, and authors of merged work keep their credit in
the commit history.

## Credits

The timing unit is shared byte for byte with [tiqora-skywater130](https://github.com/Normansrule/tiqora-skywater130). The sky130 PDK is by SkyWater and Google; fabrication would be through [Tiny Tapeout](https://tinytapeout.com).

The commit history is kept as it was written. Nothing in it has been rewritten to change who
appears as an author.
