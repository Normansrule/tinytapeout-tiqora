Chip atlas

Every module instance in Tiqora-Serial, as Yosys elaborates it from the files info.yaml submits. Ports, widths, parameters, flip-flop counts and connections come from that elaboration; what each block means comes from docs/atlas/notes.json, which docs/tools/atlas.py --check holds to the real ports and tests.

To follow a signal, open a block and choose follow next to a port: the blocks it reaches are listed, and marked ◆ in the list.
